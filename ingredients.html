Know Your Ingredients

Learn about common skincare ingredients and their uses.

Hydration

Hyaluronic Acid

Helps attract and retain moisture in the skin.

Common use: Hydrating serums and moisturizers.

Skin Barrier

Ceramides

Lipids that help support the skin's protective barrier.

Common use: Moisturizing creams.

Oil Care

Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 used in skincare for several concerns, including uneven-looking skin.

Common use: Serums and moisturizers.

Exfoliation

Salicylic Acid

A beta hydroxy acid (BHA) that helps exfoliate inside pores.

Common use: Some products for acne-prone skin.

Antioxidant

Vitamin C

An antioxidant ingredient used in skincare to improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.

Common use: Morning serums.

Moisture

Glycerin

A humectant that attracts water and helps keep skin hydrated.

Common use: Cleansers and moisturizers.

Important Skincare Tips

Introduce new active ingredients gradually. Patch test first, follow product directions and avoid combining strong actives without checking compatibility.
