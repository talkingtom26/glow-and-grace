Your Daily Skincare Routine

Simple steps for morning and night.

☀️ Morning Routine (AM)

  1. Cleanser: Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser or CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser.
  2. Serum: Optional. Choose a suitable serum based on your skin needs.
  3. Moisturizer: Cetaphil Moisturising Lotion or CeraVe Moisturising Cream.
  4. Sunscreen: Broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Apply generously and reapply as directed.

🌙 Night Routine (PM)

  1. Cleanser: Gently wash away dirt, oil and sunscreen.
  2. Serum: Optional. Use only ingredients suitable for your skin.
  3. Moisturizer: Apply a comfortable layer to hydrated skin.
